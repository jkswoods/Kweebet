Includes the mod logo in the world creation and world settings mod list.

Craft a Kweebet Workbench and build four in-world item games: Duck Race, Blackjack, Crash and Plinko. Choose supported stackable items and limits in each machine’s Settings, then use its native controls to play. All wagers and returns are virtual in-game items; there is no real-money or external-economy integration.

- Duck Race follows your marked yellow duck against three server ducks.
- Blackjack offers Hit, Stand and Double; Crash has manual Take; Plinko has eight drop choices and nine payout buckets.
- Original furniture, world displays, interface art and sound cues are included.
- The player JAR includes the MIT license and asset/third-party notices.

Built and checked against Hytale 0.7.4, with declared compatibility `>=0.7.4 <0.8.0` and CurseForge’s 0.7 tag. Later 0.7 patches have not each been tested.

Install the selected mod JAR in the Hytale Mods folder and restart. Craft the Kweebet Workbench at a stock Workbench using 8 Iron Ingots, 4 Copper Ingots and 10 Wood. Each game machine uses 6 Iron Ingots, 4 Copper Ingots and 10 Wood at the Kweebet Workbench. See the [player guide](https://jkswoods.github.io/Kweebet/) for controls, rules and recovery.

This is an early development build published on the Release channel. Back up your world and player data before installing or upgrading; use replaceable items. The actual-player full-bag reward recovery/collect-once check, a real second-client multiplayer check, and final human visual/listening acceptance remain pending. Do not rely on this build for a valuable server economy. Before 1.0, recipes, balance, behaviour and saved data may change.
