## One straightforward loop {#one-straightforward-loop}

Craft the Kweebet Workbench, make a machine, place it and press Use. Choose how many items to bet, then Race, Deal, Bet or Drop. Games start immediately and generate their own returns. No owner bankroll or lobby is needed.

Winnings go to your bag automatically while the table is open. If the bag is full, the machine retains your owned reward. Make room and reopen the table or Collect once. A lost bet stays spent.

## Preview support {#preview-support}

Use a backed-up world and replaceable items. Real second-client spectators and broad dedicated-server compatibility are still being evaluated. Normal save/reload is supported; power-loss and partial-save restoration are not certified. See [troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).
