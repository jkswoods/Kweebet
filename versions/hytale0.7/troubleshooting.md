# Troubleshooting {#troubleshooting}

## The workbench recipe is missing {#the-workbench-recipe-is-missing}

Confirm the mod is enabled for the world and Hytale is version 0.7.4. The Kweebet Workbench starts at the stock Workbench's Crafting category. Game machines are crafted at the Kweebet Workbench under Games.

## A bet is refused {#a-bet-is-refused}

Check the accepted item and your available amount. Blackjack needs an even stake; Plinko needs a multiple of four. The owner may have changed limits before your request arrived. Unsupported custom-metadata items are refused without charging the bet.

## The bag is full {#the-bag-is-full}

Free an ordinary inventory slot, then reopen the machine or use Collect. The owned reward remains at the machine until it can be inserted. Reopening does not create another reward.

## A machine cannot be removed {#a-machine-cannot-be-removed}

It must belong to you and have no active round, protected balance or owed reward. Finish the round and collect returns first. Do not delete protected machines through world editing.

## Controls or display stop responding {#controls-or-display-stop-responding}

Close the table with Escape and reopen it. If Hytale's own menus also stop accepting input, finish a normal save and restart the client. Keep the complete backup and report the game, Hytale version and what happened just before the input problem. Never force a paid game to reroll by restoring only an inventory or only a machine file.

## Preview limits {#preview-limits}

Kweebet remains a development preview. Real second-client spectators and broad dedicated-server/provider compatibility are still being evaluated. Power-loss and partial-file rollback are not certified. Use replaceable items in a backed-up world.

[Report a problem](https://github.com/jkswoods/Kweebet/issues) with the mod version and reproduction steps. Remove account information, server addresses and private player data from screenshots or logs before posting.
