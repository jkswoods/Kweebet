# Recipes

Kweebet uses stock ingots and native wood-trunk ingredient categories. Oak trunks are one accepted wood input. Recipes take four seconds each at their required bench.

| Output | Required bench | Iron ingots | Copper ingots | Wood trunks |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Kweebet Workbench ×1 | Stock Workbench → Crafting | 8 | 4 | 10 |
| Duck Race ×1 | Kweebet Workbench → Games | 6 | 4 | 10 |
| Blackjack Table ×1 | Kweebet Workbench → Games | 6 | 4 | 10 |
| Crash Console ×1 | Kweebet Workbench → Games | 6 | 4 | 10 |
| Plinko ×1 | Kweebet Workbench → Games | 6 | 4 | 10 |

One workbench and one of every game need **32 Iron, 20 Copper and 50 wood trunks** altogether. No fuel or power connection is required. Obtain the ordinary materials through Hytale's usual gathering and processing; Kweebet adds no ore or replacement currency.
